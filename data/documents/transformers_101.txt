Transformers: The Architecture That Changed NLP

Transformers are deep learning models that have revolutionized natural language processing.
Introduced in 2017 by Vaswani et al. in the paper "Attention is All You Need", transformers
have become the foundation for state-of-the-art NLP systems including BERT, GPT, and T5.

The key innovation of transformers is the self-attention mechanism, which allows the model to
focus on different parts of the input simultaneously. Unlike RNNs that process text sequentially
(one word at a time), transformers process all words in parallel, making them much faster and
more efficient to train.

Core Components:
1. Tokenizer: Converts text into tokens (subwords or words)
2. Embedding Layer: Converts tokens to dense vectors (384 or 768 dimensions)
3. Positional Encoding: Adds position information to embeddings
4. Multi-head Self-Attention: Allows model to attend to different representation spaces
5. Feed-Forward Network: Non-linear transformation
6. Layer Normalization: Stabilizes training

Self-Attention Explained:
For each word, self-attention computes:
- Query: "What am I looking for?"
- Key: "What information do I have?"
- Value: "What information should I pass to the next layer?"

By computing attention scores between queries and keys, the model learns which words are
relevant to each other. This is done in parallel for multiple "heads" (multi-head attention),
allowing the model to attend to different representation subspaces.

Advantages over RNNs:
- Parallelizable: All words processed simultaneously
- Better long-range dependencies: Can directly attend to distant words
- Scalable: Can be trained on larger datasets
- Transfer learning: Pre-trained models can be fine-tuned for downstream tasks

Applications:
- Machine Translation (Google Translate)
- Question Answering (SQuAD)
- Text Classification (Sentiment analysis)
- Named Entity Recognition
- Language Generation (GPT models)

Variants:
- BERT: Bidirectional encoder from transformer (good for understanding)
- GPT: Generative pre-trained transformer (good for generation)
- T5: Text-to-text transfer transformer (versatile)
- RoBERTa: Robustly optimized BERT (improved BERT)
